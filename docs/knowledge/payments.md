# Payments

Synthetic payment rules. A payment and an attempt are different resources.

## Posted versus received

A posted payment reduces amount due. A received payment is money that arrived and is not on the bill yet. Failed and reversed payments do not reduce amount due. Use the payment status from the tool, not the customer's screenshot.

## Failed autopay

An autopay attempt can fail and stay visible on the attempt resource even when no payment exists. A later posted payment means the failure was recovered. That recovered case is a healthy control, not a fault, when the bill shows the posted amount. Say which attempts failed and which payment posted.

## Payment not on the bill

If the customer paid and no payment row exists, the payment was not recorded. If a payment row exists but is not posted, it was not applied to the bill. Neither case is fixed by a credit. The fix is to record or post the payment. Do not propose a credit for money that was already paid.

## Late fee after the due date

The flat late fee of 50.00 INR applies only when the due date has passed and the bill is still unpaid. A posted payment that clears the bill before you explain it means you should not describe the fee as still owing. Read amount due.
