# Value-added services and plan advice

Synthetic rules for add-ons. The catalogue is small on purpose.

## A value-added service must be opted in

Caller tune and OTT Mini are optional. A monthly fee appears only when the customer opted in and the product is active. A fee with opted-in false is a charge for a service nobody took. A CSR may propose a credit for those months. Do not recommend the add-on as if it were part of the plan.

## A feature should end when the subscription is cancelled

When the subscription is cancelled, active add-ons end with it. A feature that stays active, or a fee after the end date, is a fault. Read the product termination date.

## Choosing a plan

Plan advice compares the subscription's plan with the other offerings. Suggest a cheaper plan only when the included allowance covers the usage you actually retrieved. If you did not retrieve usage, do not recommend a change. A plan change is a service change: describe it, and leave the order to a person.
