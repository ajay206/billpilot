# Roaming

Synthetic roaming rules for the learning mock. Domestic allowance is not roaming allowance.

## When roaming charges apply

Voice, data and SMS outside the home network are roaming usage. They are rated at the plan's roaming rate unless a roaming pack covers them. The usage tool shows the country and the event type. A roaming line on the bill should point at those events.

## Roaming packs

Asia roaming 1 GB and World roaming 3 GB are optional packs with a fixed data grant and a short validity. Usage inside an active pack should not also be billed as pay-as-you-go roaming. If two packs overlap, the grant must not be counted twice and must not be dropped.

## Do not auto-credit a roaming spike

A roaming spike is usage far above the same subscription's earlier cycles. Review it with the customer before any credit. Do not auto-credit a roaming spike. Open a case, keep the evidence, and wait for a person. Fraud-style flags are a signal to look, not a reason to post money.
