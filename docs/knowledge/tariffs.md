# Tariffs

Synthetic catalogue. These are the only plans the mock sells. Rates are round so a wrong rate is obvious.

## Plans in this catalogue

Smart 199 is 199.00 INR a month with 200 minutes, 2048 MB and 100 SMS. Plus 399 is 399.00 INR with 1000 minutes, 10240 MB and 300 SMS. Max 599 is 599.00 INR with 3000 minutes, 25600 MB and 500 SMS. Ultra 999 is 999.00 INR with 5000 minutes, 81920 MB and 1000 SMS. The product inventory record names the plan. The product offering lists the monthly fee and the overage rates.

## Overage rates

Voice, data and SMS above the included allowance are priced at the plan's overage rate. Data overage on Smart 199 is 0.05 INR per MB. A line priced at ten times the catalogue rate is a wrong-rate fault. Reprice it from the offering, then credit the difference. Do not quote a rate from memory when the offering tool is available.

## What the monthly fee includes

The monthly fee includes the allowance on the plan and nothing else. Roaming, value-added services and late fees are extra lines. A discount, when the subscription has one, is its own negative line.
