# Porting

Synthetic mobile number portability rules for this mock.

## How a port works

The customer keeps the mobile number and moves the subscription to another operator. Porting does not erase issued bills. Outstanding amount due is still owed. Explain any open bill before talking about the port date.

## When a port is refused

A port is refused while a collections bar is active (soft bar, hard bar, or disconnect) or while a billing dispute on the account is still open. Clear the bar or resolve the dispute first. A reminder on its own does not block a port. The copilot must not clear a bar; that is a service change for a person.

## Bills during a port

Charges up to the port date stay on the final bill. Charges after the port date should not. Treat a charge after the port the same way as a charge after cancellation: show the dates, then a CSR may propose a credit.
