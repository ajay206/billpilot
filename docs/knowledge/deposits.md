# Deposits

Synthetic deposit rules. A deposit is not a charge on the monthly bill.

## When a deposit is taken

A refundable deposit may be taken for a new postpaid account that has no payment history. The mock does not bill the deposit as a usage line. It is recorded against the account, not against a plan allowance.

## Refunding a deposit

A deposit is refunded after the account has been open and in good standing, with no open dispute and no active collections bar. The refund is a payment back to the customer, not a bill credit. Do not propose a bill adjustment to "refund" a deposit. Point the person to the deposit rule and leave the money movement to ops.

## A deposit is not credit

Using a deposit to pay an overdue bill requires a person. The copilot does not net a deposit against amount due.
