# Treatment and collections

Synthetic collections ladder. Days are counted from the invoice due date.

## The collections ladder

The standard ladder is reminder at 3 days past due, soft bar at 10 days, hard bar at 20 days, and disconnect at 45 days. A healthy unpaid account sits on the step that matches those days. Skipping a step, or barring an account that is not due, is a fault. Read the treatment resource before you describe the step. Do not guess it from the balance alone.

## An open dispute holds treatment

Opening a dispute or a trouble ticket holds an active treatment. The hold is not a credit and it does not remove a bar by itself. Status becomes held, with a hold reason. A new bar should not be applied while that hold is open. If the account was treated during an open dispute, say so and leave the unbar to a person.

## Exemptions

An exemption has a reason and a validity window. An account inside that window must not be barred. Barring an exempt account is a fault. When the window has ended, the ladder applies again.

## Barred after the bill was paid

If posted payments cover the bill and the treatment is still an active bar, the bar should have ended when the payment posted. Report the payment and the treatment stage. Do not unbar the account from the copilot.

## Promise to pay

A promise to pay, with a date and an amount, pauses the next step until that date. Ignoring a recorded promise and advancing the ladder is a fault. If the date has passed and the amount was not paid, the ladder continues.
